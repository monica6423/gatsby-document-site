## Set up

Click on Service Integration on the left to view the list of service items
![](../../img/doc/integration_dir.png)

Click SMS to enter the SMS setting page

![](../../img/doc/sms1.png)

Add information to new user/existing user to complete

- Choose a newsletter provider
  - `Default ISP SMS`: Default SMS provider, you need to specify the default in the settings of each provider
  - `SMS Direct`: SMS service provided by Signaal, without additional settings
  - `CHT SMS`: Chunghwa Telecom SMS service, you need to enter the relevant information in the provider settings
  - `TWM SMS`: Taiwan Big Brother SMS service, need to enter the relevant information in the supplier settings
  - `APTG SMS`: Asia Pacific Telecom SMS service, you need to enter the relevant information in the provider settings
  - `SMS Station`: Use your mobile phone as the SMS sending platform, you need to enter the relevant information in the supplier settings

- Please select the country code, and enter the phone number below and click Add.
