## Working hours
-   Set a weekly period for receiving alarms. Signal will only send alarms during the time you allow, and `not` during the rest of the time.
-   The default is to receive alarms 24 hours a day.

     ![](../../img/doc/shift1.png)

## Stop warning day
-   Can set special date `not` to receive alarm

     ![](../../img/doc/shift2.png)


## Extra warning day
-   You can set a special date to receive alarms

     ![](../../img/doc/shift3.png)


## Precautions
-   The extra alarm day setting will `override` the stop alarm day setting
-   Stop alarm day setting will `overwrite` working hours setting
